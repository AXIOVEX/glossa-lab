# History ledger convention — every study carries its story (2026-11-25)

Owner's order, Glossa-Lab chat, 2026-11-25: every study in this program
carries a history ledger, so that when a study is done the entire story of
how it started and how it ended can be told. Results reporting does not
carry the story; the ledger does. AEE already tracks the raw material
(claims, records, verdicts); the history ledger is the per-study narrative
layer over that material.

## The rule

1. Every study (every numbered spec, and every study-level program effort
   that lives outside a numbered spec, such as the Co-scientist rounds)
   keeps ONE history ledger, at `records/history-ledger.md` in its spec
   package. A study-level effort housed in another study's package keeps
   its own distinctly named ledger file in that package's records folder.
2. The ledger is OPENED AT REGISTRATION, in the same landing that
   registers the study. Its first entry records the commission: what was
   ordered, by whom, when, and the record the commission rests on.
3. Entries are dated and in chronological order. Each entry records the
   event or decision, who made it, what changed and why, and the filed
   record the entry rests on. Entries are added as the work happens, not
   reconstructed at the end.
4. Failures, corrections, and superseded approaches are entries like any
   other; a ledger that records only successes is not a ledger.
5. The closing entry states how the study ended (or, for an in-flight
   study, its state at the time of writing) and rests on the closing
   record.
6. A ledger assembled after the fact is marked BACKFILLED at its head,
   names the sources it was assembled from, and marks every place the
   filed record is silent as a GAP. Narrative, motive, and sequence are
   never invented to fill a gap.
7. The ledger narrates; it does not revise. No result, verdict, standing,
   or claim changes by being written into a ledger. Corrections to a
   study's substance go through that study's own records discipline, and
   the ledger then records that they happened.

## Backfill of 2026-11-25

Ledgers for Specs 090-104 and for the Co-scientist rounds were backfilled
under this convention on 2026-11-25, each marked BACKFILLED with its
sources named and its gaps marked. The Spec 106 ELSEWHERE framework
specification carries the formal standard this convention follows; where
the two texts differ in wording, the convention's rules above govern
repo practice.
