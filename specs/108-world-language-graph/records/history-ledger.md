## 2026-11-27 — DECISION (population honesty)

Population is bounded by what the ingestion source and
the filed program record actually support. The research
report is a tertiary web-index synthesis (source grade
P4 at extraction); its consensus / contested / fringe
labels map to the graph's confidence labels without
upgrading (CONSENSUS → WELL_SUPPORTED, CONTESTED →
POSSIBLE, FRINGE → SPECULATIVE), and its ranges are
stored as ranges. Items a batch cannot support are
quarantined with reasons in the batch report, never
padded. The filed program record governs the three
priority programs wherever it differs from the report
(design §6.2, B6).
