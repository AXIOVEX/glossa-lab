# Custody supplement — erratum (Red Team Round 2, RT2-14)

Filed 2026-11-21. This erratum corrects
`records/custody-supplement-2026-11-21.md` in two respects. The
supplement is not edited; this record governs where the two
differ. Adjudication: `RED-TEAM-ROUND-2-ADJUDICATION.md` (goal
record), RT2-14 and RT1-28.

## 1. The undertaking's objects

The supplement §2 extends the objection undertaking to "the
Stage 4.4 outcome record and its results file" as records
depending on "the acquired corpus". That is the wrong object
set, in both directions. The exposure the undertaking answers
is the benchmark's already-published Otten-derived derivatives.
The undertaking's objects are: **the benchmark outcome record
(`records/meroitic-benchmark-outcome-record-2026-11-20.md`),
`records/leg-b-results.json`, `records/leg-c-summary.json`, and
citations of them.** If Otten or Anastasopoulos objects, those
are the records the program undertakes to withdraw or correct.

## 2. The acquired corpus's separate basis

The acquired corpus is the Kovetsky build — code MIT, data
CC BY 4.0 (`records/corpus-completion-record-2026-11-20.md`
§2) — and is not Otten-derived. Stage 4.4 computed on that
corpus's held-out partition (see the Stage 4.5 supplement
correction record of this date, §1). An Otten/Anastasopoulos
objection does not govern the acquired corpus, and Stage 4.4's
custody needs no undertaking under the custody record. Where
Stage 4.4's custody is discussed, this separate, licensed basis
is the statement of record.

## 3. Header reference

The supplement's header cites
`records/corpus-custody-and-license-2026-11-20.md`, which does
not exist. The custody record is
`records/custody-record-2026-11-20.md`.
