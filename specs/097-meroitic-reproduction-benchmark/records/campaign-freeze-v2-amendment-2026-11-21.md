## The defect

Freeze v2 freezes the R1/R2/R3 tests, the decision rule, and
the seals. It names no generation rule. The Stage 4.3 screen
that actually produced the six candidates — at least 10
development occurrences; filiation-adjacency ≥ 0.10 computed
on the development corpus; pool of forms with ≥ 10
occurrences, ≥ 3 letters, not in the 182-form vocabulary —
appears for the first time in the Stage 4.3 outcome record
itself. The screen's parameters were fixed at execution,
registered nowhere before the candidates existed. The
"blind" discipline that was in fact observed (the screen ran
on the development corpus and its published reference
materials only) held for the held-out side; it did not hold
for the screen's own parameters.

It is also recorded here that the pool rule "not in the
vocabulary" is a rule about a file, not about knowledge: the
182-form vocabulary is not the anchor register, and three of
the six surviving candidates (soreyi, asoreyi, wisi) are
variants of forms in anchor freeze v1, Class B. See the
citation-discipline record of 2026-11-21 §6.

## The thresholds, registered now — post hoc, and labelled so

For the record, the Stage 4.3 screen as executed:

- Development occurrences ≥ 10.
- Filiation-adjacency ≥ 0.10 (development corpus).
- Pool: forms with ≥ 10 development occurrences, ≥ 3 letters,
  not in the 182-form vocabulary file.

These values are registered **after the fact**. No claim is
made that they were frozen before the screen ran; the
amendment's purpose is that the executed values stand on the
record under their own names, so the Stage 4.4 scores can be
read against the screen that produced their candidates.

## Binding rule for future stages

Any future candidate-generation stage in this program
registers its screen thresholds in a freeze or amendment
record **before** the screen runs, or the stage's outcome
record opens with the statement that its thresholds were
fixed at execution. The six candidates' Stage 4.4 scores
stand as executed; this amendment does not re-open them.
