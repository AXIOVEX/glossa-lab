## 1. The dissemination copies named

On a refusal trigger under §4 of the protocol, the motions of
§§4.3–4.5 extend to the released Spec 097 paper and every copy
of it the program has disseminated, namely:

1. **The Zenodo versions** of the paper, under concept DOI
   10.5281/zenodo.24449061 — every version record in that
   chain, not only the current one.
2. **The Academia listing** of the paper (the work page
   carrying the current version's attachment).
3. **The ResearchGate publication** of the paper.
4. **Supplementary material** deposited with any of the above
   or distributed with the paper.
5. **Public summaries** of the paper on surfaces the program
   controls (the program's public research pages and any
   summary the program itself has posted).

## 2. Treatment, by whether a version can be removed

- **Copies the program can remove or replace directly** (the
  Academia attachment, supplementary files under the program's
  control, and the program's own public summaries): the §4
  withdrawal and annotation motions apply to them directly,
  on the §4 timetable.
- **Copies a venue does not let the program remove** (a
  superseded Zenodo version, which the venue's versioning
  keeps addressable; a ResearchGate copy once distributed):
  the program (a) files a linked notice — a new version,
  correction notice, or listing annotation, as the venue
  permits — that links the copy to the refusal record and
  states that the copy is withdrawn; and (b) sends a
  repository notification to the venue (Zenodo, ResearchGate,
  or Academia, as applicable) requesting removal or
  annotation of the copy, with the refusal record as the
  stated basis. The notice, the notification, and the venue's
  response are filed with the refusal record in the same
  motion as the withdrawal, and the §4 timetable governs the
  program's own acts; a venue's delay is recorded, not
  treated as compliance or as refusal.

## 3. Scope

This supplement governs the paper's dissemination copies only.
Derived artifacts, citations, and published aggregates remain
governed by §§4.4–4.5 as adopted; the audit-record preservation
of §4.6 is unchanged; the scope limit of §4.9 is unchanged.
