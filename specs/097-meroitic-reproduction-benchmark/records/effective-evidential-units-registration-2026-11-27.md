counts from the held build's files, and state exactly what

**Leg 2 — Cross-split dependencies on the Stage 4.4 basis.**
Extending OD-17's Stage 4.2 audit to the Stage 4.4 held-out
basis, examine whether material on the held-out side of the
Stage 4.4 split is dependent on material on the development
side, under three dependency definitions, each stated
before it is applied:

- **(a) Inscription identity** — the same REM inscription
  contributing material to both sides.
- **(b) Near-duplicate text** — distinct strings that are
  near-duplicates under a similarity rule stated in the

- **(c) Formula family** — texts sharing a formula template
  (the funerary formula families and any other family the
  freeze enumerates), where family membership is assigned
  by a rule stated in the freeze.

For each definition, the leg reports the number of
held-out units with a development-side dependent, and the
effective number of independent held-out units that
remains. The nominal held-out count of record is the
Stage 4.4 basis as filed; this study measures it, it does
not re-run Stage 4.4 and it changes no Stage 4.4 result.
