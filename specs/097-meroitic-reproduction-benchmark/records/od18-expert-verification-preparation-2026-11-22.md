Bounded, itemised; each item takes confirm / challenge
with cause / no position, the tranche's existing
instrument:

1. Our scoring uses glosses from the published tradition,
   and your 2016 survey counts 39 lexemes as confirmed in
   meaning. Is it right to treat those 39 as confirmed for
   scoring purposes, and if not, which ones should come
   off that list?
2. Five glosses are used as scoring controls: qore
   "ruler, king", kdi "woman, lady", ste "mother", mk
   "god", and ato "water". Do you confirm each of the 5,
   or would you challenge any of them?
3. The inventory reads the funerary invocation as Isis
   (wosi) and Osiris (asiri, also written soreyi), and
   treats the filiation forms as fixing the mother
   relation (tede, "born of") and the father relation
   (terike, "begotten of") while the segmentation of
   those forms stays debated. Is that a fair statement of
   where things stand?
4. The closing clause of the funerary formula is carried
   in the received reading as "he/she was good", and the
   inventory flags it as the formula's least pinned slot.
   Should it stay flagged that way, or is there a better
   current reading?
5. The inventory carries qes "Kush" as provisional and
   excludes it from scoring controls, because of the
   qes/qos variation. Is provisional the right status
   for it?
6. Since 2012 and 2016, has the standing of any item in
   the published wordlist changed in a way that should
   stop us using it as an anchor?
