# Citation-discipline record — erratum (Red Team Round 2, RT2-26)

Filed 2026-11-21. This erratum corrects two citations in
`records/citation-discipline-record-2026-11-21.md`. The record
is not edited, except its §4 heading, which was broken across
two lines and is repaired in place at this touch, as the

## 1. A8's basis (§1 of the record)

The record's §1 states that A6 and A8 "are the vocabulary
header counts of the dim-100/120 embedding files". For A6 that
stands. For A8 it does not: the benchmark outcome record's Leg
A table gives A8's primary basis as **distinct whitespace types
in mero-corpus.txt, cross-confirmed by the dim-100/120
Meroitic vocabularies**. The embeddings cross-confirm A8; they
are not its basis. Citations of A8 follow the outcome record's
basis statement.

## 2. The B1 gloss pair (§6 of the record)

The record's §6 attributes the gloss pair 'wos "Isis", sorey
"Osiris"' to freeze v1's B1. That wording is freeze **v2**'s B1
(`records/campaign-freeze-v2-2026-11-20.md`). Freeze v1's B1
(`records/meroitic-benchmark-freeze-2026-11-20.md`) reads "wosi
(wise) … asiri (soreyi)". The §6 substance — that the B1
glosses are the freeze's own registered forms — stands; the
quotation is from freeze v2, and citations follow this erratum.
