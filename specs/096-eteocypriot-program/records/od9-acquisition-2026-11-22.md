## Identification of record

1. **The Androkles syllabic sides.** Antoine Hermary and
   Olivier Masson, "Inscriptions d'Amathonte IV", *Bulletin
   de correspondance hellenique* 106 (1), 1982,
   pp. 235-244. DOI 10.3406/bch.1982.1914 (Crossref;
   publisher of record PERSEE Program). Dedication A, the
   digraphic and bilingual dedication of King Androkles to
   the Cyprian Aphrodite (Limassol Museum AM 649;
   SEG 32.1318 A), is the article's no. 66, per the article's
   own heading. The sign-level transcriptions of the
   syllabic sides stand at pp. 236-238. Dedication B was
   first published by Hermary & Hellmann 1980 (BCH 104,
   p. 262), with an improved reading in the 1982 article
   (SEG 32.1318 B). Some secondary bibliographies cite the
   article as pp. 235-242; the Persee and Crossref range,
   235-244, is the range of record here.

2. **Perna 2019.** Massimo Perna, "La grande inscription
   d'Amathonte (ICS 194 + 195) : une nouvelle etude
   epigraphique. Rapport preliminaire", in Anna Cannavo and
   Ludovic Thely (eds.), *Les royaumes de Chypre a l'epreuve
   de l'histoire: transitions et ruptures de la fin de l'age
   du Bronze au debut de l'epoque hellenistique*, Bulletin
   de correspondance hellenique, Supplement 60, Athens:
   Ecole francaise d'Athenes, 2018 (the program cites it as
   Perna 2019), pp. 213-220. ISBN 978-2-86958-307-8 (print).
   DOI 10.4000/books.efa.3031 (Crossref, book chapter,
   confirmed against the volume's table of contents, which
   places the chapter at p. 213, the next chapter beginning
   at p. 221). The chapter is a new edition from autopsy in
   the Louvre of the great Amathus inscription (Louvre
   AM 799a and b): 48 of 216 signs read differently from
   Masson, altering 24 words, with the resulting reading
   printed at a glance at the chapter's end.
