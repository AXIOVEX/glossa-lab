## What this stage establishes, and what it does not

The three sandboxes exist as registered: faithful strips of the pinned sources, built from the floor's admitted inputs only, with derived L1 inventories that two independent implementations reproduce. That is the whole of it. The inventories are filed in the raw output beside, and never merged into, the programs' working sign lists.
