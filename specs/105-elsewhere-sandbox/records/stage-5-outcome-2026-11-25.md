## What this stage establishes, and what it does not

The derived meaning system now exists inside each sandbox as a filed object: one role record per mark assembling everything the ladder has derived about that mark, the partition of the inventory into roles, and the graded overlap between roles, reconciled exactly against the counting, order-structure, and substitution rungs below it. The headline result is a null at the registered standard, stated plainly: at the full-profile standard — same count, same attested successors, same attested predecessors, same positions — **no two marks share a role in any of the three sandboxes** (372 unique roles over 372 marks, 35 over 35, 32 over 32). What the systems have instead is graded partial agreement, filed pair by pair in the overlap tables: agreement on three of four components occurs 375 times among Indus pairs, 12 times among Eteocypriot pairs, and once among Meroitic pairs, where the digit pair {6, 9} — the one exact substitution class the ladder found at L4 — is separated by frequency alone. A looser identity standard (context without frequency, or overlap promoted to classes) would merge roles, but it is not the standard this stage registered, and no merged role is claimed here; whether such a standard should be registered is a later question, on this filed result. That is the whole of it. L5 is the last derivation rung of the ladder: every rung registered in spec.md section 5 below the gates has now been derived in all three sandboxes. Above this stage stand only the boundary gates of L6 (G-ETE, G-MER, G-IND), none of which is commissioned by this stage; the reserve remains sealed for G-IND's own stage; and the role tables are filed in this spec's records, beside and never merged into the programs' working sign lists. Any gate requires its own commission under the spec.
