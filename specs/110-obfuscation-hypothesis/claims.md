# Spec 110 — Claims

Six claims, C110-1..C110-6, each with its falsifier
and its does-not-establish line, in the program's
claim form. All six were intaken at registration
through the sanctioned importer (spec.md, tasks T003)
from aee-claims.json. Intake sets no standing, and no
claim in this package carries a result beyond what
its test cases check. The claims are the study's
design and outcome claims — that the battery as filed
is the battery the registration specifies, that its
gate ran before any scoreable verdict, and that each
corpus's filed verdicts are the mechanical consequence
of the registered rules — not claims that any script
was or was not deliberately obfuscated.

**C110-1 — The battery as filed is the test the
registration specifies.** The four signature families
of spec.md §3 (distribution shape; stratum asymmetry;
substitution structure; inventory anomalies), the
common machinery of the registration §1, the bands of
§3, the control gate of §4, the verdict rules of §5,
and the kill criteria of spec.md §7 are the
deliberate-obfuscation test of this study, and no leg
of this study runs under any other design.

*Falsifier:* a leg of this study executed under a
design other than its registration; a threshold
adjusted after the gate ran; a verdict filed that is
not the mechanical consequence of the registered rule
over the filed figures; a signature of this study
presented anywhere as proof of deliberate intent.

*Does not establish:* that any script was deliberately
obfuscated, that the battery can detect every form of
obfuscation (spec.md §2 registers a class it cannot),
or that a signature's absence disproves the
hypothesis beyond the registered floors.

**C110-2 — The gate ran first.** The battery's
control validation (registration §4: detection,
invariance, and false-positive legs) executed and
passed before any real-corpus verdict of this study
was scored; had any leg failed, no real-corpus verdict
would have been filed as scoreable.

*Falsifier:* a real-corpus verdict of this study filed
as scoreable while any gate leg stood failed or
unrun; a gate figure that does not recompute from the
filed raw output.

*Does not establish:* that the battery is sensitive to
obfuscation forms outside its four registered
families, or that its thresholds are the only
defensible ones — only that they were frozen from
controls before the targets were scored.

**C110-3 — Indus verdicts.** The Indus corpus's
per-family verdicts filed in the execution record are
the mechanical consequence of registration §5 over
the filed Indus figures (derivation set, 1,336
artifacts, 5,611 tokens; site strata as registered).

*Falsifier:* a filed Indus verdict that does not
follow from the registered rule over the recomputed
figures; an Indus figure that does not recompute from
the pinned corpus under the registered handling.

*Does not establish:* that the Indus script was or was
not deliberately obfuscated; a PRESENT verdict is a
candidate finding for adjudication, with its
alternative explanations filed beside it.

**C110-4 — Eteocypriot verdicts.** The Eteocypriot
corpus's per-family verdicts filed in the execution
record are the mechanical consequence of registration
§5 over the filed Eteocypriot figures (the Spec 096
corpus of record, primary Amathus inscriptions).

*Falsifier:* a filed Eteocypriot verdict that does not
follow from the registered rule over the recomputed
figures, including a verdict other than NOT TESTABLE
AT THIS POWER where a registered floor is unmet.

*Does not establish:* anything about Eteocypriot
production beyond the battery's reach at this corpus
size; the corpus's small size is itself the binding
fact the verdicts record.

**C110-5 — Meroitic verdicts.** The Meroitic corpus's
per-family verdicts filed in the execution record are
the mechanical consequence of registration §5 over
the filed Meroitic figures (376 texts, 28,734 tokens
after the registered damage-marker handling; family B
NOT TESTABLE as registered, the build carrying no
stratum metadata).

*Falsifier:* a filed Meroitic verdict that does not
follow from the registered rule over the recomputed
figures; a Meroitic band computed with Meroitic
control samples inside it (the leave-target-out rule
of registration §3).

*Does not establish:* that the Meroitic corpus was or
was not deliberately obfuscated; its deciphered
script makes it the battery's nearest natural-writing
neighbour, not an exception to the rules.

**C110-6 — Standing and provenance discipline.** No
outcome of this study moves any standing, verdict, or
tally in any program; every PRESENT verdict is filed
as a candidate finding with its alternative
explanations stated; every run of this study carries
its full provenance (harness pin, seeds, data pins)
and replays from its filed records.

*Falsifier:* a standings record altered by this
study's landing outside its own outcome records; a
candidate finding of this study presented as a
conclusion; a filed figure that does not replay from
its records under TC-110-03.

*Does not establish:* that the candidate findings, if
any, will survive adjudication — that is the gates'
work, not this study's.

## The revision's claims (2026-11-27)

Five further claims, C110-7..C110-11, filed with the
revised battery's registration
(records/revision-registration-2026-11-27.md) as the
bundle claims-revision.json and intaken through the
same sanctioned importer. The first battery's six
claims stand unamended; the revision narrows the
claim set where its development record required it
(C2 retired, §4.4 of the revision registration) and
adds no claim the first battery's forms do not
support.

**C110-7 — The revised battery is the test the
revision registration specifies.** Family A keeps
statistics A1–A4 under per-system bands with two
separately gated patterns (flattening, controls
T-HOM and T-POLY; structure, control T-SHUF); family
B is carried over unchanged; family C is C1 alone
with C2 retired on the filed development record;
family D is the joint inventory-inflation rule
(inventory V and the fixed-rank tail mass, both
above their per-system bands, control T-HOM).

*Falsifier:* any statistic, band, gate leg, or
verdict of the revision computed otherwise than as
the revision registration specifies; any threshold
carried over in revision registration §1 found
loosened; a pooled cross-system band deciding any
verdict.

*Does not establish:* that the revised battery
detects deliberate obfuscation in any real corpus;
it establishes only what test was registered.

**C110-8 — The revision gate ran first.** The gate
of revision registration §5 ran before any real
corpus was scored, on the registered seeds; C1
counts as re-registered only if its false-positive
leg holds; a family or pattern that failed its gate
at a profile rendered no verdict there, and the
gate record says so.

*Falsifier:* a revision gate record absent or
postdating the revision execution record; a verdict
filed from a family, pattern, or profile whose gate
leg failed; a false-positive count above the
registered rule for a family or pattern that
rendered verdicts; a gate figure that does not
recompute under TC-110-07.

*Does not establish:* that any gate outcome is
correct beyond the filed recomputation.

**C110-9 — The Indus outcome under the revised
battery is the filed outcome.** Per-family verdicts
where the revision gate licensed them at the Indus
profile, and the gate failure where it did not, as
filed in records/revision-execution-2026-11-27.md.

*Falsifier:* a filed Indus verdict that does not
follow from the revised registered rule over the
recomputed figures (TC-110-08); an Indus figure
that does not recompute from the pinned corpus
under the registered handling; a verdict filed from
a gate-failed family, pattern, or profile.

*Does not establish:* that the Indus script was or
was not deliberately obfuscated, or anything about
intent; a secrecy regime can exist with no textual
signature, and a cipher device can exist without a
secrecy function.

**C110-10 — Eteocypriot is not testable at this
power under the revised battery.** Every family
files NOT TESTABLE AT THIS POWER on the primary
Amathus corpus (11 sequences, 54 tokens), which
meets no family's registered floor.

*Falsifier:* any verdict other than NOT TESTABLE
AT THIS POWER filed for the Eteocypriot corpus
under the revised battery; a corpus floor applied
otherwise than as registered.

*Does not establish:* anything about Eteocypriot
writing practice; the corpus is far below every
floor, as the first registration anticipated.

**C110-11 — The Meroitic outcome under the revised
battery is the filed outcome.** Per-family verdicts
where the revision gate licensed them at the
Meroitic profile, the gate failure where it did
not, and family B NOT TESTABLE AT THIS POWER (the
corpus build carries no stratum metadata), as filed
in records/revision-execution-2026-11-27.md.

*Falsifier:* a filed Meroitic verdict that does not
follow from the revised registered rule over the
recomputed figures (TC-110-08); a Meroitic figure
that does not recompute from the pinned corpus
under the registered handling; a verdict filed from
a family or pattern whose revision gate leg failed
at the Meroitic profile; a family B verdict other
than NOT TESTABLE AT THIS POWER.

*Does not establish:* that Meroitic was or was not
deliberately obfuscated, or anything about intent;
a secrecy regime can exist with no textual
signature, and a cipher device can exist without a
secrecy function.
