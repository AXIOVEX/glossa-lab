## The distinction

Spec 101 §1's research question asks whether the endings
the ICS 196 development text contains **recur**, at the
predicted positions, in the great Amathus inscription. The
binding floor rule (floor amendment §1.3) sets each floor
at the upper tail of Binomial(M, p_i) with p_i = f_i / D —
the development frequency itself is the null rate. The
rule therefore detects occurrence **above** the development
frequency (enrichment), not ordinary recurrence at that
frequency. Under the registered rule, an ending that
recurs at essentially its development rate will usually
return NOT SUPPORTED (family-wise chance of any candidate
passing under exact rate transfer is bounded by Q < 0.05).

0.008559 (0.021224 at 13); 3/11 → floor 18, tail 0.012130
(0.027075 at 17). Counts of 8 and 11 respectively — repeated
occurrence close to the development rates — would fail
these floors.
