## 1. What this act does

The adjudication dispositioned every recommendation of the Academia Co-scientist audit of the 94 strict-core readings and adopted the replacement class scheme; it adjudicated, and execution in the standings records is a separate act citing it. This record is that execution. From this act forward, the standings of the 94 in the program's current-standings statements are the executed classes in section 2. Dated filings are not rewritten; where a dated filing carries the retired labels, it is corrected by appended note or by the living surfaces that cite this act, under the program's append-only record rule.

Class vocabulary, as adjudicated: **E** external-support claims on file, unverified, NOT IDENTIFIABLE; **I** internally motivated candidate, reading-level support not demonstrated, identifiability untested; **R** requires revalidation; **P** expert-pending, retained provisionally (no named expert or referral is documented for any of the three); **U** insufficient evidence. The two candidate classes are never summed as supported.

## 4. Addendum — M202 (can): the contradicted external claim, settled

**Question.** The adjudication (Q1 row 4) placed M202 in class E on the audit's recommendation. The re-verification pass, landed after the adjudication was drafted, checked the claim behind that standing against its named source and returned CONTRADICTED: Mahadevan's 2009 *Vestiges of Indus Civilisation in Old Tamil*, read in full, contains no reading 'can' for any Indus sign, no sibilant discussion, and no mention of Shu-ilishu or of a /su/ slot. Whether a contradicted claim keeps class E was flagged at that landing for this execution. It is settled here, by rule, not by exception.

**Rule.** A class E standing rests on its filed external-support claim while that claim stands unverified. When the program's re-verification checks the claim against its named source and the source contradicts it — the claim is not in the source — the claim is struck and the reading leaves class E. The reading is then reclassed on its remaining filed basis: class I if a filed internal candidate motivation remains, class U if none remains. Two neighbouring outcomes do not strike a claim: partial verification (some legs verified as filed, others unverified or failing on a checkable part) leaves the reading in E with the partial outcome recorded on its row, and non-verifiability (the claim could not be checked from lawfully reachable material) leaves the reading in E with NOT VERIFIABLE recorded.

**Consistency applications, same rule.** M047 stays E (VERIFIED AS FILED, provenance only). M124 stays E (PARTIALLY VERIFIED, DEDR legs only). M329 stays E (PARTIALLY VERIFIED: the dictionary leg checks out as filed; the filed Sanskrit pura comparison fails on its checkable part, recorded on its row in the re-verification record, and does not strike the claim under this rule because the claim's remaining legs stand as filed or unverified, not contradicted in their named sources). M044 stays E (NOT VERIFIABLE from lawfully reachable material). M330 stays E (NOT VERIFIABLE: the held Parpola 1994 text is an OCR scan, and that record states a negative result from it is weaker than a contradiction from a clean text; if a clean text later contradicts the claim, this rule moves M330 at that time).

**Application to M202.** M202's class E basis was the Mahadevan 2009 attribution alone: four mentions of one publication are one source (Q4 flag S), and its falsifier-annex entry states the independent standing "rests entirely on that outside evidence". The claim is struck under the rule. No filed internal candidate motivation remains: the step from 'can' to partial coverage of the /su/ phonological slot is unspecified in the filed summary (adjudication Q1 row 4), and no positional count, dictionary entry, or battery result is filed for this sign. **M202 therefore takes class U (insufficient evidence).** This is a finding about the citation as filed; it does not decide what value, if any, this sign carries.
