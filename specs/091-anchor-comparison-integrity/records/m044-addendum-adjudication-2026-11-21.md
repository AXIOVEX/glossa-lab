## The rule

A claim is struck when re-verification checks it against its
named source and the claim is not in the source; the reading
is then reclassed on its remaining basis (I if an internal
motivation remains, U if none). Partial verification and
non-verifiability do not strike.

   external-support claim stands verified as filed and at least
   one leg is contradicted by its named source, the claim is
   struck.** Recorded as an interpretation of the landed rule,
   governing future cases until the owner rules otherwise.
