## 2. Q2 — The word "supported" for the internal-only 65 (6 points)

1. APPLY. Unqualified "supported" is not defensible as a reading-level claim on these materials. The identifiability failure of the six does not by itself defeat support for the 65; what defeats an unqualified endorsement is the absence of inspectable, value-discriminating results and the conflation of lexical matches, positional regularities and reading validation.
2. APPLY. Vocabulary ruling: "supported" survives only in the narrow sense "internally consistent and not refuted, but not identified" — in the audit's fuller qualification, internally supported as a candidate-generating hypothesis under the program's assumed language, sign interpretation and grammar, with the specific sound/meaning assignment not independently established. Because most readers will miss that limitation, the program label is I (internally motivated candidate) for every one of the 65, individually enumerated in section 1. This does not imply equal evidence or equal promise.
3. APPLY. Keep inference layers separate: a crosswalk maps catalogue identities, not sound values; a dictionary establishes a word or cognate set, not that a sign encodes it, nor automatically a Proto-Dravidian reconstruction or Harappan-era availability; position/bigrams constrain structure, not an exact lexeme; motif association concerns text-image relationship, not necessarily denotation; expert review assesses an argument and a favourable opinion alone would not identify a reading.
4. APPLY. Correction adopted: the corrected promotion battery is a structural screen, not reading validation. All 19 re-promoted signs — M004, M013, M014, M017, M018, M020, M026, M027, M030, M034, M041, M051, M057, M060, M063, M073, M080, M089, M391 — have positional pass + bigram pass with DEDR not_run; that pattern restores a structural screen, not the recorded sounds. Conversely M012, M039, M059, M077, M162, M233, M305, M328, M336 score one; their missing and failed components must not be conflated. M048 has no corrected battery result filed. No screening outcome alone establishes or refutes its particular reading.
5. APPLY. Counted evidence lines are not demonstrated independence. Exemplars adopted as named: M004, M014, M017, M018, M020, M026, M027, M030, M034, M041, M089, M391, M077, M162 (multiple counted lines with GRAMMAR as the only listed category, or limited category coverage); M151 counts three lines while listing DEDR and GRAMMAR; M267 counts four while listing three categories. Several observations can share one category, so these are not necessarily arithmetic errors, but neither multiple labels nor repeated runs establish independent information; four mentions of one publication are one source, not four replications.
6. APPLY WITH MODIFICATION (credit for a same-day program act). The audit found no filed per-reading falsifier for any of the 94 — a serious operational gap, not proof of unfalsifiability in principle; each reading needs an outcome that would count against its stated sound, meaning or sign relationship, with an advance decision rule, and adding a falsifier improves future testing without retroactively supplying support.
