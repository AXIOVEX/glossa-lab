# Indus program refresh: reassessment of the 94

**Assessment date:** 22 November 2026  
**Materials:** `uploads/indus-REFRESH-BRIEF.md` and `uploads/indus-APPENDIX-STATE.md`.

**Scope:** This is an assessment of the supplied account, not an independent replication of its reported acts. I have not inspected the inference log, frozen specifications, source publications, execution artifacts, or version-12 manuscript. References below to findings mean findings *reported in these two documents*. The earlier audit itself was not supplied; I do not assume details beyond those reproduced here.

## 1. R1 — Reassessment of the standings

**The reported work improves the register's auditability substantially; it does not yet establish a sign reading.** The corrected sourcing, failed claims, and non-promotions are material improvements over an unqualified support vocabulary. The arithmetic of the current classes is consistent: **3 E + 66 I + 4 R + 3 P + 18 U = 94**. These remain administrative categories, not ordered levels of evidential strength or estimates of correctness.

### 1.1 E is defensible as a provenance flag, not an evidence grade

The intended distinction is now explicit: an external-support *claim* is recorded, not necessarily verified. On that definition, **M330 can honestly remain E**, provided its “not verified; OCR search negative” outcome is displayed beside the label wherever the label appears. An isolated E would invite an inference the record does not warrant.

**M124 and M329** illustrate another limit: verifying that a proposed lexeme exists does not verify its attachment to an Indus sign. Their dictionary-leg successes must not be summarized as partial verification of the sign reading. For M329, the corrected dictionary number repairs a citation; it supplies no new sign-to-value evidence, and the failed Sanskrit comparison must cease contributing to the basis.

The class description **“unverified, NOT IDENTIFIABLE”** conflates separate questions. Verification is component-specific; identifiability depends on the prediction and comparison actually tested. Use separate fields for external-claim status and identifiability outcome. “No reading identified by the executed study” is justified; a blanket claim that every E reading is intrinsically unidentifiable is not.

### 1.2 I is appropriately modest, but its identifiability wording is stale

“Internally motivated candidates; support not demonstrated” is fair. **“Identifiability untested” is no longer a sufficient common description** after Stage 2. Record the executed outcomes individually:

- 48 lacked power under this design;
- 17 had no testable neighbour prediction in their filed bases;
- M267, M347, M375, and M089 qualified for sealed scoring;
- none has yet been discriminated by this study.

Keep the execution denominator of 69 and its membership distinct from the current 69 E/I entries. These counts are not a new partition of today's register without a membership crosswalk. In particular, **M048's new I membership does not establish that it underwent the historical screening**.

For the 17, “NOT IDENTIFIABLE by construction” should always carry the qualifier **under the filed prediction model**. Missing a neighbour prediction is a limitation of the submitted hypothesis representation, not proof that the reading could never be identified by other evidence. For the 48, low power is neither corroboration nor refutation.

### 1.3 M047 exposes the difference between record compliance and evidence

The itemization rule addresses a real defect: a class should be traceable to an explicit basis item. **M047's E-to-U move is procedurally intelligible under that rule. It is not an evidential downgrade produced by new contrary evidence.** The supplied record says its prior-proposal provenance was verified; omission from the inference-log entry does not undo that verification.

The clean settlement is to preserve both facts: **current U under the filed-basis rule; verified prior-proposal provenance in a separate field**. If the verified proposal is eligible as a basis item, allow a dated, source-linked amendment under the same rule available to every sign. Do not silently repair the historical log. Neither amendment nor E reinstatement would validate the reading.

This is also why E and U should not be read as a ranking: M330 can satisfy E's documentary criterion while M047, with stronger verified provenance, remains U for a filing reason.

### 1.4 R, P, U, and the M048 move need narrow interpretations

- **R — M087, M165, M264, M311:** revalidation is a task status. It should not imply that a formerly valid reading awaits routine renewal.
- **P — M222, M362, M398:** “expert-pending” suggests an actual review arrangement. With no named expert documented, use “expert review needed; no reviewer documented.” Neither coder outreach described in the brief establishes expert review of these readings.
- **U — including M202, M044, M047, M342, and M176:** means the retained basis is insufficient. It does not mean the reading has been proved false. In particular, exact reproduction of M342 and M176's counts is not reproduction of their proposed readings; the reported p-values do not themselves establish either reading.
- **M048:** the corrected score and separate adjudication justify reporting that a procedural threshold was met. The supplied summary does not reveal what the two points measure or whether their inputs satisfy the no-single-tradition promotion rule. Publish that check beside the move. The I label is modest enough; treating the score as two independent pieces of confirmation would require evidence not supplied here.
- **M099:** it is candid to flag that no specific falsifier can be extracted from the filed gloss. Do not invent one to reach 94/94. Mark the candidate as insufficiently specified until its basis can be stated.

Finally, **93 recorded falsifiers are not 93 demonstrated falsification tests**. Their existence closes a documentation gap; their specificity and execution determine their evidential value.

## 2. R2 — The rule for contradicted external claims

**Retain the adopted rule: strike the contradicted support item and reclassify the reading on what remains.** Do not retain a known-false claim as active E support merely because it remains historically on file. Preserve it in the audit history with its disposition.

Apply the rule at the level the contradiction actually reaches:

1. A wrong entry number may be a repairable citation error if the intended entry is recoverable and genuinely supports the stated lexical claim.
2. A wrong gloss or a misattributed proposal invalidates that particular support item.
3. Loss of an external item does not automatically defeat another independent basis, and does not prove the proposed sign reading false.
4. Reclassification follows the remaining admissible basis. U is appropriate only when that basis is insufficient; I may be appropriate when genuine internal motivation remains. E can remain only if another qualifying external claim survives, with its verification status disclosed.

**M202:** on the supplied account, the named publication does not contain the attributed reading. Striking that attribution and moving to U is appropriate given the reported absence of a sufficient remainder. The conclusion is “the cited source does not support this attribution,” not “the source disproves /can/.”

**M044:** the reported dictionary mismatch, absence of a verified surviving leg, and insufficient remainder support U. Checking the printed edition was an appropriate test of the stated reopening condition; on the supplied result that condition did not occur. The proposed /ku/ value is unsupported by the filed basis, not thereby disproved in every possible derivation.

**M329:** correcting 4524 to 4570 is a different operation from retaining the false Sanskrit gloss. Repair the former with an amendment trail; strike the latter. The distinction prevents a general rule from treating clerical mistakes and substantive failures as equivalent.

**M330:** failure to find an attribution in OCR is presently reported as nonverification, not demonstrated contradiction. That distinction is warranted. Its status may change after targeted inspection of the relevant source locations or better text; it should not be declared contradicted merely to make it resemble M202.

## 3. R3 — Ranked next work

### Priority 1 — Seek independently fixed sign-to-language evidence

**What it would settle:** whether the program can move from patterns compatible with a reading system to evidence that selects linguistic values over serious alternatives.

Prioritize a small number of explicit mappings whose evaluation does not depend on values inferred by the same program. Fix the prediction and relevant rival interpretations before examining new evidence. Independent evidence need not mean simply another corpus: a corpus interpreted through the existing assignments can reproduce the same dependence.

**Affected readings:** potentially any E/I candidate, or a currently U reading with genuinely new evidence. No class moves merely because this search is commissioned. Blind extraction of Parpola's assigned values contributes to provenance checking, not to this bridge.

### Priority 2 — Resolve Stage 3's execution prerequisites, then close the frozen study once

**Recommendation:** conditional go for the four historically eligible signs, not a fresh scoring exercise over today's 69 entries. The absence of a genuine holdout limits the result; it need not prevent finishing a bounded internal diagnostic. Conversely, there is no scientific reason to unseal an invalidly specified comparison merely to close a workflow.

Before release, publish a short, dated eligibility reconciliation against the frozen specification:

- **Information reuse:** retain the pseudo-held-out ceiling and document what the seal protects. Sealing after candidate generation cannot erase the candidates' prior exposure to both corpora.
- **Power:** retain the 48 low-power outcomes. Do not relax the token floor after observing that only four candidates proceed.
- **Prediction specification:** retain the 17 non-testable filed models. Verify that each of the four eligible candidates has a fixed, executable prediction and rival comparison without unrecorded free choices.

Two sign-specific checks matter. **M267's Stage 3 model must not count the excluded slot-pattern argument as independent confirmation.** Eligibility cannot rehabilitate its failed dictionary leg. **M089's inconsistent filed upgrade counts must be reconciled with the actual Stage 3 inputs.** Neither defect automatically invalidates an otherwise independent frozen statistic; the supplied summaries do not establish whether that separation holds.

Keep the frozen 10-nat margin, 2-nat free-choice penalty, and 99th-percentile permutation criterion. Check that nuisance choices are treated consistently for the candidate and rivals, and disclose how the study handles selecting among four eligible signs. Do not invent a new correction after seeing results.

If a prerequisite requires changing a frozen model, record that model as not executable under the original design and register the replacement separately. Do not silently substitute **M048 for M047** or treat matching cohort sizes as matching populations. Unseal once for valid original comparisons under the custody rules.

**What it would settle:** whether M267, M347, M375, or M089 discriminates among the specified rivals under this reused-data design. Success would remain **pseudo-held-out discrimination**, not an identified reading, independent confirmation, or automatic promotion. Failure would challenge the tested prediction; its force depends on power and on how specifically the reading entails that prediction.

### Priority 3 — Finish source checking where it can change a live basis

Work first on unresolved *sign-to-value attributions*, not additional confirmation that a word exists.

- **M330:** inspect the most likely source locations and record search coverage and OCR limitations. A clean contradiction would trigger the strike rule; verification would improve provenance only.
- **M124 and M329:** resolve the outside-proposal and corroboration claims, beyond their dictionary legs.
- **Unresolved Elamite forms and the incomplete Mahadevan citation:** map each to named signs and basis items, then finish or explicitly retire the claim. The supplied documents do not identify those affected signs, so I cannot responsibly assign them here.
- **M047:** settle the dated log amendment as described in R1, without presenting it as new evidence.

Accept the blind coders' work as independent extraction of what a source says. It does not become independent support for a reading merely because an outside linguist performs it.

**What it would settle:** attribution accuracy and the survival of specific E claims. It could move E entries to I or U after a strike, or repair documentary status; it cannot alone validate a linguistic value.

### Priority 4 — Deposit the iconography join and rerun the motif claims

Deposit the per-seal labels for the stated 1,670-seal population, with source provenance, missing-label handling, and the seal-ID join. A complete join makes the computation reproducible; it does not guarantee the labels were independently assigned. Distinguish genuinely blind recoding from reconstruction informed by the claimed associations.

Replay the original 13 claims and their original thresholds before conducting revised exploratory tests. Report the comparison family and sensitivity to missing labels or duplicate artifacts. Keep the failed **M063** specification visible; a redesigned analysis cannot retroactively turn its reported lift of 4.35 into success against 5.0.

**What it would settle:** whether the reported sign–motif associations exist under their stated definitions. It may restore or remove basis items for the 13 affected readings. Even a replicated association does not uniquely supply a sound value or lexical gloss.

### Priority 5 — Use the completed graph audit to constrain claims, not certify independence

The supplied citation form is appropriately qualified on its main point: **0 class changes is a property of these class rules**, not proof that dependencies are harmless. The E result is explicitly structural; I can remain I despite losing much of its basis because the category asserts so little.

Retain **44 weakened bases, or 63 when ambiguous premises are included**. These are alternative totals, not additive findings. “Acyclic” excludes directed cycles in the encoded reading-premise graph; it does not exclude shared-source dependence, reused training information, or common candidate-system assumptions. Likewise, “210 primary evidence items” is an item-type census, not 210 independent confirmations.

For a current citation, label M047's missing external item as the historical defect that motivated the rule, with its subsequent U disposition. State which current E memberships are protected by the audit's class rules. This avoids leaving the reader with the impression that M047 remains an immovable E case.

Next inspect the **25 ambiguous score items** for unstated inputs, concentrating on any used in promotions or Stage 3 models. Publish a basis-survival view for affected readings rather than commissioning another headline class-stability audit.

**What it would settle:** whether recorded scores hide dependencies and whether specific retained bases remain usable. It could affect M048's promotion rationale or the four Stage 3 models if they use such scores; that connection requires inspection, not assumption. The graph result alone warrants no promotion.

## 4. R4 — Evidence that would change this assessment

### Toward greater confidence

The largest change would come from **independently constrained sign-to-language mappings that make successful, specific predictions against serious rivals**, with the relevant choices fixed before evaluation. One well-supported bridge could matter more than many internally compatible glosses.

A second substantial change would be predictive performance on material not involved in constructing the assignments, with independent transcription or coding and an explicit account of residual overlap. Performance must distinguish the proposed values, rather than merely recover sign order or generic positional structure.

Successful Stage 3 results would make a smaller, bounded change: they could demonstrate discrimination within the frozen pseudo-held-out comparison. Verified source attributions, reproducible motif counts, and corrected dictionaries would raise confidence in the record and particular basis items. They would not independently identify readings.

### Toward lower confidence in particular candidates

Clean source contradictions, failure of a sufficiently powered frozen prediction, or loss of an allegedly independent basis after input tracing would reduce confidence in the affected claim. The reported M202 and M044 strikes already exemplify the first kind. Low power, inaccessible data, and incomplete OCR searches should remain unresolved evidence rather than be converted into negative findings.

### Toward judging the approach misdirected

I would distinguish a candid early-stage program from a method that cannot identify its target. The stronger adverse assessment would be warranted if:

- after adequate-power tests and explicit rivals, incompatible value assignments repeatedly perform equivalently, while the method offers no independent means of selecting among them;
- successful sequence or motif predictions consistently depend on structural patterns shared by many linguistic and nonlinguistic assignments, leaving the proposed values dispensable;
- enough changes of gloss, segmentation, direction, or sound correspondence are permitted after outcomes that no prospective failure can count against a reading;
- newly claimed independent evidence repeatedly turns out to inherit the same assignments or candidate-generation inputs.

Repeatedly concealing failed tests or changing frozen rules after unsealing would separately undermine trust in the program's execution. The supplied record instead reports several failures and preserves them; those reports should not be treated as evidence of misconduct or, by themselves, evidence that the whole approach is futile.

## Overall verdict and permissible public strength

**Place weight on these standings as a candid, versioned register of candidate status and unresolved work—not as a ranking of reading correctness.** The supplied account gives more reason to trust the correction process than the previous vocabulary did. It does not give corresponding new reason to regard the sign values as established.

The strongest defensible public formulation, based on these materials, is:

> We maintain a register of 94 candidate Indus sign readings. Its classes describe filed bases and review status, not calibrated probabilities of correctness. Source checks have rejected some attributions, while other claims remain unverified or unreproducible from the tracked record. The executed identifiability screening has identified no reading; four candidates qualified for further sealed scoring, whose maximum interpretation is discrimination on pseudo-held-out data. The Indus script is not deciphered, and no reading in this register is established.

Do not use **“independently supported,” “validated across independent corpora,” “dependency-independent,”** or an unqualified **“supported readings”** for these standings. Even **“internally consistent and not refuted”** should be applied per reading and per surviving claim, not blanket-applied to a register containing documented failed basis items. The supported conclusion is that the program has improved its account of uncertainty; the central identification problem remains open.
