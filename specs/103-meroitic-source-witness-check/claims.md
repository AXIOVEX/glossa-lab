# Spec 103 — Claims

Four claims, C103-1..C103-4, each with its falsifier
and its does-not-establish line, in the program's
claim form. The claims are intaken at registration
through the sanctioned importer (spec.md, D-103-2);
intake sets no standing, and no claim in this package
carries a result: the check has not executed.

**C103-1 — The frozen extraction preserves the source
distinctions and text-presence counts needed for
these candidate decisions.** (The Co-scientist
session's stated claim, Round D unit D-M-3, carried
verbatim.) For the six Stage 4.4 candidate forms
(soreyi, asoreyi, xolkete, pxolkete, xolkte, wisi),
the frozen extraction's text-presence counts on the
held-out partition (spec.md §2) equal the counts the
registered witness protocol (spec.md §3) derives from
the acquired build's source witnesses at every
decision-bearing locus, and no adjudicated correction
changes either leg of any form's frozen prediction
(presence in at least 3 held-out texts; held-out
funerary share within [0.75, 1.0]).

*Falsifier (the session's kill check, verbatim):* a
demonstrated source/extraction discrepancy that
crosses a registered decision boundary kills the
claim for the affected candidate. If adjudicated
source corrections leave every relevant threshold
outcome unchanged, the input-error explanation of the
failures is retired within the checked scope. If
witnesses or search coverage are insufficient, the
outcome for the affected candidate is NOT CHECKED /
INCOMPLETE COVERAGE, not verified absence.

*Does not establish:* that the acquired build's
transcriptions are true to Griffith's printed
editions — the printed page is not machine-readable
here (corpus completion record §4, gap 5), and the
check's witnesses are the build's own second
representations (spec.md §2, D-103-4) — or that any
candidate reading is correct, or that a sparse
failure, its inputs verified, means anything beyond
the failure of the frozen prediction Stage 4.4
already recorded.

**C103-2 — The split membership is fixed throughout
the diagnostic.** The execution recomputes the basis
and partition from the pinned build by freeze v2 §1's
rule (exact dedup of the `text` field; held out iff
SHA-256(text) mod 10 == 0) and verifies the frozen
counts (368 distinct texts; 335 development / 33 held
out; strata as freeze v2 §2); every witness correction
is applied to token counts within that frozen
partition, and no corrected text is rehashed and no
text changes side.

*Falsifier:* a recomputed partition differing from
freeze v2 §2's counts in any cell; an execution step
that assigns a text to a side by any rule other than
the frozen rule applied to the frozen (uncorrected)
text; a corrected text whose hash is recomputed for
split purposes.

*Does not establish:* that the frozen split was well
chosen — only that this diagnostic holds it fixed, so
its result answers the extraction question and not a
re-split question (the session's registered reason:
correcting text and rehashing it would answer a
different question).

**C103-3 — No pooling, and no score-driven
correction.** The xolkte-family forms (xolkete,
pxolkete, xolkte) are checked and reported as
distinct forms; no variant is pooled into another
form's count, and no spelling variant is counted
toward a candidate form (freeze v2 §7's varietas
discipline). A correction enters the adjudication
only where the witness comparison demonstrates a
discrepancy, and every correction is filed with its
witness evidence (inscription, both token readings,
classification).

*Falsifier:* any pooled xolkte-family count anywhere
in the outcome record; any correction in the outcome
record without a filed witness divergence behind it;
any token counted toward a candidate form on the
ground that it is a spelling variant of it.

*Does not establish:* that the family's forms are
unrelated — only that this diagnostic does not let
their relationship move a count, exactly as the
session's no-pooling rule requires.

**C103-4 — The diagnostic moves no standing by
itself.** Whatever the check finds, Spec 097's frozen
Stage 4.4 outcome (1 PARTIALLY SUPPORTED, 5
UNSUPPORTED on held-out), its verdicts, and its
tallies stand unchanged on their own records; a
demonstrated boundary-crossing discrepancy licenses
only a separately registered corrected-input
sensitivity result, and a CLAIM STANDS outcome
licenses only the interpretation statement of
spec.md §5.

*Falsifier:* any record of this study stating a
changed Stage 4.4 score, verdict, or tally; any
citing record treating this diagnostic's outcome as
a re-score of Stage 4.4.

*Does not establish:* that the frozen outcome is
correct in substance — only that this diagnostic is
an interpretation of its provenance, and that the
program's discipline keeps the historical outcome
and any corrected-input sensitivity as separate,
separately dated results.
