# Spec 112 — Claims

Seven claims, C112-1..C112-7, each with its falsifier
and its does-not-establish line, in the program's
claim form. All seven are intaken at registration
through the sanctioned importer (spec.md, tasks
T003) from aee-claims.json. Intake sets no standing.
The claims are the study's design and discipline
claims — that the study as filed is the study the
registration specifies, that its preflight gates all
scoreable output, and that its outputs are candidates
with provenance — not claims that any induced
structure is correct.

**C112-1 — The study as filed is the study the
registration specifies.** The corpus pins of spec.md
§2, the three induction targets of §3, the preflight
of §4, the execution sequence of §6, and the kill
criteria of §7 are the Meroitic structure-induction
study of this spec, and no leg of it runs under any
other design.

*Falsifier:* a leg of this study executed under a
design other than its registration; a corpus other
than the pinned corpus scored; a floor or threshold
adjusted after the preflight ran; an output filed
that is not the mechanical consequence of the
registered induction over the filed pins.

*Does not establish:* that the induction's outputs
are correct, that Meroitic has the structure the
targets presuppose, or that any structure found is a
fact about the language rather than a candidate
about the corpus.

**C112-2 — The preflight gates all scoreable
output.** The conformance preflight of spec.md §4
(P-1 segmentation recovery, P-2 formula-slot
recovery, P-3 partial validity with its shuffle
control), judged against the registered floors by
independent recomputation, runs and passes in full
before any corpus output of this study is scoreable.

*Falsifier:* a scoreable corpus output filed before
a full preflight pass; a preflight figure that the
independent recomputation does not reproduce; a
floor met by redefinition after the run.

*Does not establish:* that a passed preflight makes
the induction's corpus outputs true — it licenses
them as candidates, nothing more — or that the
ground-truth sets are beyond challenge by the
program's expert-verification routes.

**C112-3 — Segmentation outputs are ranked
candidates with provenance.** Each segmentation
candidate this study files carries its score, its
rank, and its full generation provenance (search
state, seed, data pins), and is filed as a candidate
for adjudication, never as a reading of the form.

*Falsifier:* a filed segmentation without its
provenance; a segmentation presented anywhere as the
form's established analysis; a candidate set filed
without its margins.

*Does not establish:* that the top-ranked
segmentation of any form is its morpheme structure,
or that Meroitic morphology is agglutinative in the
way the target's form presupposes.

**C112-4 — Recurrent partials are distributional
objects.** Each partial this study files carries its
support, its positional profile, and its host-form
set, and is claimed as a recurrent distributional
object in the pinned corpus only.

*Falsifier:* a partial filed without its support or
positional profile; a partial identified anywhere in
this study's records as a morpheme, an affix with a
function, or a word.

*Does not establish:* that a recurrent partial is a
morpheme, that it carries a meaning or a grammatical
function, or that its recurrence is not an artefact
of the corpus's formulaic composition — the
alternative the shuffle control and the slot analysis
exist to bound.

**C112-5 — The formula-slot grammar is induced
blind and compared, not fitted.** The slot grammar
of spec.md §3 is induced from the funerary text
stream without the Class B template as an input;
the template enters only at the preflight (P-2) and
at the filed comparison of the induced grammar
against it.

*Falsifier:* the Class B template, or any labeling
derived from it, present in the induction's inputs;
an induced grammar whose filed form was edited
toward the template after the run.

*Does not establish:* that the induced slots are the
formula's functional slots, that the template's
received readings are correct, or that slot structure
in the funerary stratum generalises to the corpus's
other genres.

**C112-6 — No gate of Spec 102 or Spec 109 Stage 5
is consumed or altered.** This study is
corpus-internal structure only: it supplies no
vocabulary to Spec 102, changes no gate or source
rule of Spec 102, assigns no gloss to any form, and
leaves Spec 109 Stage 5 gated on Spec 102 exactly as
registered.

*Falsifier:* an output of this study entered as an
input to Spec 102's vocabulary, gates, or scoring,
or to Spec 109 Stage 5, without a new registration
deciding that use on its own record; a gloss
assigned to a Meroitic form anywhere in this study's
records.

*Does not establish:* that this study's structures
could never legitimately inform those studies —
only that any such use is a separate, registered
decision, not a consequence of this one.

**C112-7 — Failure closes the study as filed.** If
any preflight leg fails its registered floor, or
the shuffle control yields structure above its cap,
the study closes with the failing leg and the
counted shortfall on the record, and the response
is a revised design under a new registration, never
a lowered bar.

*Falsifier:* the study continuing past a failed
preflight leg; a floor lowered, a control weakened,
or a ground-truth set re-cut after a failure, within
this registration.

*Does not establish:* that a closed study falsifies
structure induction for Meroitic in general — a
closure is a finding about this design at this
corpus, in the kill-criterion discipline's own
terms.
