## The counts as filed

The custody extraction reports attested forms covering
**14 scored concepts in total: 7 outside the twelve anchor
concepts and 7 inside them**, against the registered G-1
requirement of **40 scored concepts with at least 20 outside
the twelve**, per family test.

## The sharpened arithmetic

Considered separately, the threshold deficits are 26 total
concepts (40 − 14) and 13 outside concepts (20 − 7). That
separate framing understates the binding requirement:

- Any 40-concept set contains **at most 12 inside concepts**
  (there are only twelve anchor concepts), and therefore
  **at least 28 outside concepts**.
- Retaining the reported 7 outside concepts therefore
  requires **at least 21 additional outside concepts**, even
  if all five missing inside concepts become available and
  eligible.
- Adding only 13 outside concepts plus those five inside
  concepts would yield 32 concepts, not 40.

The figure of record for planning is therefore **at least 21
additional outside concepts**, superseding the custody
extraction's "13 outside" shortfall framing. These are
best-case lower bounds on additional eligible, attested,
scored concepts — not on forms or spellings; actual
requirements can be greater after source-admissibility and
comparability filtering.

## Per-family qualification

The gate machinery evaluates each family test separately. A
union count across sources or families does not demonstrate
40 usable concepts in each test, and the arithmetic above
must be satisfied per test, not only in the union.
