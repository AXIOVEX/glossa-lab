## 2. What S-1 is

Per the registration record and spec.md, S-1 is the fuller published Meroitic wordlist of the scholarly tradition: Rilly 2010, *Le méroïtique et sa famille linguistique*, and the lexicon apparatus of Rilly & de Voogt 2012. Digitised by the program into a new vocabulary, S-1 forms the Meroitic side of the study's decisive leg. Its authority basis is Element 7 of the OD-14 chain (filed at `specs/097-meroitic-reproduction-benchmark/records/od14-chain-supplement-s1-2026-11-22.md`): published scholarly sources not yet held; digitisation is a separate owner-gated acquisition act; the compilation, once made, is the program's own work over published sources, with no redistribution of the lexicon text and scoring/aggregate use only.

The spec's registered rule stands: if S-1's acquisition is refused, unaffordable, or yields too little, Leg A is not administered, and no substitute source may be quietly introduced. Rilly's 2003 EPHE thesis is an earlier, different text and was NOT pursued as a substitute.

## 3. Exact editions identified

1. **Claude Rilly, *Le méroïtique et sa famille linguistique*.** Afrique et Langage 14 (SELAF 454), Peeters, Louvain-Paris, 2010. 557 pp., paperback, French. **ISBN 978-90-429-2237-2 (ISBN-10 90-429-2237-0).** Load-bearing contents: Chapter III compares 43 Meroitic words (pp. 59–156); the Meroitic lexique is at pp. 114–147; the 200-term Northern East Sudanic comparison apparatus is at pp. 420–529.
2. **Claude Rilly & Alex de Voogt, *The Meroitic Language and Writing System*.** Cambridge University Press, print publication 27 August 2012. 262 pp. **Hardcover ISBN 978-1-107-00866-3 (ISBN-10 1-107-00866-2); eBook ISBN 978-1-139-56053-5.** The "lexicon apparatus" the spec names is its Appendix (pp. 189–230): two hundred terms used for the comparison of the languages within the NES group, with etymologies and proto-NES reconstructions, the full discussion referenced to Rilly (2010: 420–529). Verified from the publisher's Cambridge Core listing page, 2026-11-26.
