# Spec 102 — Claims

Five claims, C102-1..C102-5, each with its falsifier
and its does-not-establish line, in the program's
claim form. The claims are intaken at registration
through the sanctioned importer (spec.md, D-102-2);
intake sets no standing, and no claim in this package
carries a result: the study has not executed.

**C102-1 — The expanded-wordlist R1 test separates
Nubian/Nara affiliation from chance at its registered
bar.** On the vocabulary built from the digitised
published wordlist (spec §3.1, S-1), attestation-
filtered against the acquired inscription corpus
(S-2), the R1 family test's primary statistic T
(spec §3.2) passes its registered bar — Holm-adjusted
permutation p below 0.01 across the two-test family —
when Leg A is administered under gates G-1 and G-2
(spec §3.7); if it is administered and fails, the
failure excludes true correspondence shares above the
freeze's computed consistency boundary (spec §4).

*Falsifier:* Leg A administered with both gates met
and the R1 test's Holm-adjusted p at or above 0.01
with an observed T at or below the freeze's
consistency boundary for θ = 0.30 at d = 0.75 — or
any administration of Leg A with a gate unmet, which
voids the administration rather than supporting the
claim.

*Does not establish:* that any single Meroitic
reading is correct, or that the affiliation, if
supported, holds at strengths below the design's
consistency boundary — the claim is a family-level
discrimination at registered power, not a decipherment.

**C102-2 — The expanded-wordlist R2 test separates
Afroasiatic affiliation from chance at its registered
bar.** The same construction as C102-1, against the
R2 comparator set (spec §3.2), reported with its own
per-test concept set and denominators.

*Falsifier:* as C102-1, for the R2 test.

*Does not establish:* that a failure of both family
tests supports the isolate hypothesis — R3 is
recordable only as not displaced (spec §5), exactly
as freeze v2 §5 of Spec 097 registered.

**C102-3 — The study is administered only at
registered power, or closes NOT ADMINISTERED.**
Leg A executes only when gates G-1 (at least 40
scored concepts per family test, at least 20 of them
outside the Stage 4.2 twelve) and G-2 (freeze-time
simulation showing at least 80% power at θ = 0.30,
d = 0.75 for the primary statistic) are both computed,
filed, and met before any scoring; otherwise the
study closes NOT ADMINISTERED with the failing gate
and the counted shortfall on the record, and no
outcome of an unadministered study is reported as a
verdict on affiliation.

*Falsifier:* a scoring run of Leg A whose freeze
lacks either gate's computation, or whose gate
figures do not recompute from the frozen lists; a
NOT ADMINISTERED closure reported anywhere in the
program's records as evidence about affiliation.

*Does not establish:* that a NOT ADMINISTERED closure
is a failure of the program — it is the design's
registered refusal to produce an uninterpretable
verdict, per unit M-18's instruction.

**C102-4 — No scoring uses an unstated or
mislabelled table.** Every mapping table,
denominator, and rule used by any scoring run of
this study is written in full in the study's freeze
before the run, and every ASJP gloss label in the
freeze is verified against ASJP parameters.csv under
its pinned hash (spec §3.8).

*Falsifier:* a scoring run whose mapping, denominators,
or labels differ from the freeze's, or a freeze table
whose labels the parameters.csv check rejects — the
defect OD-17 flagged as F-A against freeze v3 §3 of
Spec 097, closed here by construction.

*Does not establish:* that the tables are correct in
substance — only that they are completely stated and
label-verified, so a re-runner needs nothing the
record does not contain.

**C102-5 — The decisive leg rests only on clarified
material, and the Otten leg only on a clarification.**
Leg A computes only on sources whose authority the
OD-14 chain records as clarified, or on the program's
own compilation of published scholarship (spec §3.1);
Leg B executes only after a filed OD-14 clarification
covering the use, publishes no Otten-derived text,
and a refusal under OD-14 §4 suspends this spec with
the rest of the program.

*Falsifier:* a Leg A input whose authority state is
unclarified in the OD-14 chain; a Leg B run filed
without the clarification it cites; any Otten-derived
text in a published output of this study.

*Does not establish:* that the clarified sources are
accurate — the Kovetsky corpus's transcription-quality
caveat (OD-14 Element 2) carries over every result
this study produces.
