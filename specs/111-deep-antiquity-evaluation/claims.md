# Spec 111 — Claims

Eight claims, C111-1..C111-8, each with its falsifier
and its does-not-establish line, in the program's claim
form. All eight were intaken at registration through
the sanctioned importer (spec.md, tasks T003) from
aee-claims.json. Intake sets no standing. The claims
are the study's evaluation claims — the grades of the
four predictions as the evaluation record derives
them, and the discipline claims about how the
evaluation was run — not decipherment claims, and not
claims that the evaluated hypothesis is impossible.

**C111-1 — The evaluation as filed is the evaluation
the registration specifies.** The hypothesis statement
of spec.md §1, the four predictions of §2, the grading
rule of §3, and the two evidence legs of §4 are the
evaluation of this study, and no grade in this study
was entered under any other design.

*Falsifier:* a grade entered in this study's records
that is not one of P1–P4 as spec.md §2 states them; a
grade entered without its decisive evidence cited in
the evaluation record; a record of this study
presenting the hypothesis as assumed, as dismissed by
posture, or as proven impossible.

*Does not establish:* that the four predictions
exhaust what the hypothesis could predict under a
different formulation, or that the grading scale is
the only defensible one — the claim registers this
evaluation's design and its discipline, not the
hypothesis's fate under every formulation.

**C111-2 — P1 (cross-system inheritance) is NOT
SUPPORTED.** The documented derivations give the
three systems three separate, local, mutually
unrelated origins (Meroitic from Egyptian hieroglyphic
and demotic forms; the Cypriot syllabary from
Cypro-Minoan in the Linear A tradition; the Indus
script from local antecedent marks with no demonstrated
external parent); their numerical practices share no
convention beyond the tally principle; and the
distributional measurement filed at
records/p1-measurement-raw-output.json finds no
clustering of the three systems' structural profiles
relative to the program's deciphered control and a
matched synthetic null (within-trio mean distance
3.819 vs trio-to-controls mean 3.643, on the filed
feature set).

*Falsifier:* a demonstrated shared derived feature
set (sign forms with values, structural conventions,
or numerical practices) among two or more of the three
systems, traceable to a common ancestor and exceeding
what chance and independent invention predict; or a
re-run of the filed measurement under its filed
feature set showing the trio clustering against the
controls.

*Does not establish:* that no contact ever occurred
between the cultures (contact is documented, e.g.
Indus–Mesopotamia trade, and is not common descent),
or that a paleographic comparison could not be run
with data the program does not hold (C111-7).

**C111-3 — P2 (developmental discontinuity) is NOT
SUPPORTED.** Each script has local developmental
precursors in the stratified or documentary record:
Indus antecedent marks in the Ravi phase (c. 3300–2800
BCE) continuing through the Kot Diji phase (c. 2800–
2600 BCE) into the mature script at Harappa; the
Cypriot syllabary preceded by roughly five centuries
of Cypro-Minoan on the same island; the Meroitic
script preceded by centuries of Egyptian writing in
Kush and derived from Egyptian forms.

*Falsifier:* a demonstration that a named phase in
one of these precursor sequences does not exist, is
misdated out of sequence, or does not connect to the
script it precedes (e.g. that the Ravi/Kot Diji marks
bear no relation to the mature Indus signary, shown
against the excavated corpus).

*Does not establish:* that development was smooth,
continuous in every generation, or uninfluenced by
contact — only that the precursor trails P2 requires
to be absent are present.

**C111-4 — P3 (chronological anomaly) is NOT
SUPPORTED.** No script in the program and no
demonstrated direct ancestor of one is securely dated
materially earlier than the standard chronology
allows; the earliest Indus antecedent marks (c. 3300
BCE) are contemporary with proto-cuneiform and inside
the standard account; and the claimed older writing
cases (Jiahu marks, Vinča symbols, the Dispilio
tablet) stand in the scholarly record as sign use or
proto-writing not demonstrated to encode language,
none of them ancestral to a program script.

*Falsifier:* a written artifact — signs in sequence in
a system ancestral to a program script — from a
sealed, independently dated context materially before
3400 BCE, published with its dating and its ancestral
relation demonstrated; or a peer-reviewed
demonstration that one of the three labeled cases is
a language-encoding script ancestral to a later
tradition.

*Does not establish:* that no older sign use existed
(it demonstrably did), or that an earlier find is
impossible — only that none currently meets the
prediction's terms.

**C111-5 — P4 (content residue) is NOT SUPPORTED
overall.** The earliest readable texts of the
Eteocypriot and Meroitic traditions carry ordinary
content within their cultures' documented attainment
(the Amathus bilingual's civic-religious dedication;
Meroitic royal and funerary formulae on Egyptian-
derived templates). The Indus leg is UNTESTABLE in
content while the script is unread, and unsupported
in form: the corpus's brevity (derivation-set mean
4.2 signs per text) cannot carry the treatise content
P4 requires.

*Falsifier:* a demonstrated reading of an earliest-
stratum text of any of the three traditions encoding
astronomical, mathematical, or calendrical knowledge
beyond that culture's independently documented
attainment at that date.

*Does not establish:* anything about the content of
the unread Indus corpus beyond its format limits, or
about later texts of any tradition (P4 grades the
earliest stratum, where transmitted knowledge would
appear first and strongest).

**C111-6 — The distributional measurement is as
filed.** The measurement at
records/p1-measurement-harness.py, run on the Spec
105 custody token streams and the Spec 107 Latin
donor text under its declared six-feature set, returns
the profiles and distances filed at
records/p1-measurement-raw-output.json (SHA-256
6022f09a…): within-trio mean distance 3.819,
trio-to-controls mean 3.643, Meroitic–Latin the
closest program-system pair at 3.130; and its limits
(small system count, one real control, the
Eteocypriot derivation subset of 14 texts / 69 tokens,
Latin sentence-texts) are stated on its face.

*Falsifier:* a re-run of the filed harness over the
pinned custody inputs returning materially different
profiles or distances; a filed limit found understated
against the custody data (which triggers spec.md §6
kill criterion 3).

*Does not establish:* that a richer feature set or a
larger control set could not find structure this
measurement misses — it is a descriptive, low-power
leg, and the claim is about its fidelity, not its
sufficiency.

**C111-7 — P1's paleographic leg is UNTESTABLE ON
CURRENT EVIDENCE.** The program holds no comparable
sign-shape dataset (form features with provenance)
for the three systems and their documented parents,
so no shape-similarity measurement was run; the
evaluation record §5 states what acquiring the data
would take, including that Indus sign images are
statistics-only under the CISI terms this program
observes.

*Falsifier:* production of a program-held, comparable
sign-shape dataset for the three systems that this
evaluation overlooked.

*Does not establish:* that the paleographic question
is unanswerable in principle, or that the published
derivations (which are substantially paleographic
arguments) are weak — only that the program cannot
currently run the leg itself.

**C111-8 — The closing accounting is as filed.** The
evaluation record §7 states what the hypothesis
explains that the standard account does not (near-
simultaneous emergence and recurrent Neolithic sign
use, both of which the standard account also
addresses), what it costs in unsupported assumptions
(a pre-3400 BCE literate civilization leaving no
written artifact; a transmission channel crossing
more than eight thousand years unattested; recipient
traditions independently producing the local precursor
trails the standard account predicts), and the single
most decision-changing piece of new evidence (a
sealed, independently dated pre-3400 BCE written
artifact demonstrably ancestral to a program script).

*Falsifier:* a material cost or explanatory gain of
the hypothesis, on evidence available at the
evaluation date, that the accounting omits.

*Does not establish:* that the balance could not
change with new evidence — the accounting is dated
2026-11-27 and is re-gradable on any find of the kind
C111-4's falsifier names.
