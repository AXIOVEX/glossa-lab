# Spec 099 — Stage 2 design note: reporting mandate for any future Q3 re-run

Filed 2026-11-21 under Red Team Round 3 finding RT3-17
(adjudicated APPLY WITH MODIFICATION: the remedy is this
recorded note carried to the Stage 2 gate) and Round 4
finding RT4-14, which found the mandate absent from this
package. The landed audit already satisfies the mandate's
substance; this note makes it prospective and binding on
any future re-run.

## The mandate

Spec section 4.2's class rule is binary: a reading's class
changes only when a treatment's removal leaves it with no
basis at all. A class count alone therefore cannot express
partial dependence, and a future re-run of the Q3 treatment
(section 4.1 removals, section 4.2 recomputation), over
this log or any successor log, **must report the
weakened-basis count alongside the class count, in both
treatments** — the number of readings whose bases lose any
item, with the losses named per reading — in its headline,
not only in its tables. A re-run reported as a class count
alone does not meet this package's reporting standard.

The landed audit's headline is the model: class changes 0
of 94 under both treatments; bases weakened 44 of 94 under
the primary treatment, 63 of 94 under the sensitivity
treatment.

## The scope note that travels with it

Any future citation of the Q3 result, by a re-run or
otherwise, carries the Round 4 scope note appended to the
completion record (RT4-01): the E quarter of the no-change
result is structural under section 4.2's E rule, and the
definition question it raises (flag F-9) is the owner's.
A re-run under a changed class-E definition is a new audit
under a new rule, and must say so on its face.
