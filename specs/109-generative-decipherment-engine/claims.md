# Spec 109 — Claims

Nine claims, C109-1..C109-9, each with its falsifier
and its does-not-establish line, in the program's claim
form. All nine were intaken at registration through the
sanctioned importer (spec.md, tasks T003) from
aee-claims.json. Intake sets no standing, and no claim
in this package carries a result beyond what its test
cases check. The claims are the study's design claims —
that the engine as filed is the engine the registration
specifies, and that each part, when executed, returns
exactly what its registration licenses — not claims
about any script or language.

**C109-1 — The engine as filed is the discovery side
the registration specifies.** The five generative parts
registered in spec.md §3 and in
`records/registration-2026-11-27.md` §§3–7 (assignment
search; sign-class and structure induction; the context
channel; Meroitic gloss induction; Eteocypriot reading
candidates), the shared conformance preflight of
spec.md §2, the separation of generation from
adjudication, the provenance discipline of §8, and the
kill criteria of §9 are the discovery side of this
study, and no leg of this study runs under any other
design.

*Falsifier:* a leg of this study executed under a
design other than its registration; a generated
candidate entering any record without its full
generation provenance; a candidate of this study
presented anywhere as a reading, a gloss, or a
decipherment rather than as a ranked candidate with
margins; a kill criterion firing on the record without
the affected part closing.

*Does not establish:* that any part will generate a
correct candidate, that the search spaces contain the
truth, or that generation at these corpus sizes can
succeed — the claims register a capability and its
discipline, not an outcome.

**C109-2 — Assignment search (Part 1).** Under the
registration filed at
`records/registration-2026-11-27.md` §5, the Indus
assignment search — constraint propagation from the
register's E-standing anchor values and Part 2's
induced positional classes, then simulated annealing
over the registered value inventory under registered
restarts and seeds, optimizing duplicate-aware
cross-validated predictive likelihood — returns a
ranked candidate set of at most the registered K = 20
assignments, each carrying its penalized margin under
Spec 098's frozen decide() rule computed once on the
sealed 109 reserve against the frozen permutation null
and the structural-only rival, and the leg's outcome is
the mechanical consequence of the frozen bar over those
margins: at least one candidate DISCRIMINATED (or
IDENTIFIABLE ON HELD-OUT, the reserve being a genuine
holdout), or SEARCH NEGATIVE AT THE FROZEN BAR, or
INDETERMINATE AT THIS POWER where the reserve's floors
are unmet.

*Falsifier:* a candidate scored on any material other
than the once-opened 109 reserve; a margin computed
under any rule other than the frozen decide() rule; a
free choice left uncharged at the frozen 2 nats; the
reserve opened more than once, or opened before the
search freeze's hash commitment; a filed outcome that
is not the mechanical consequence of the registered bar
over the filed margins; the bar lowered, in this study
or by amendment, in response to a negative outcome.

*Does not establish:* that a DISCRIMINATED candidate is
a correct assignment — it establishes that the
candidate passed the program's frozen identifiability
gate on genuinely held-out material, and enters
adjudication as a candidate; correctness of any sign
value remains for the program's standing machinery,
and a negative outcome is a finding about the search
and the information available, not about the script.

**C109-3 — Structure induction (Part 2).** Under the
registration filed at
`records/registration-2026-11-27.md` §3, the induction
over the full corpus (390 marks, 1,670 artifacts)
files its positional classes, segmentation boundaries,
affix-like recurrent partials, and numerical /
metrological subsystem analysis as generated structure
hypotheses in the study's generated-hypotheses
register, each with full generation provenance and its
registered stability results (split-half and
out-of-stratum), and each then facing the existing
gates (anchor comparison; identifiability) under those
gates' own registrations — induction itself moving no
standing, anchor, or class.

*Falsifier:* an induced structure filed without its
provenance or without its stability results; an
induced structure whose stability checks failed filed
as passing; an induction output altering any standing,
anchor, or class other than through a filed gate
outcome under that gate's own registration; a structure
tuned after its stability failure and refiled without
a new registration.

*Does not establish:* that any induced class,
segmentation, or partial is a fact about the script —
each is a generated hypothesis whose standing, if any,
is decided by the gates it is filed to, and the
numerical subsystem analysis establishes at most that
the counted contexts behave as a constrained system
under the registered arithmetic checks, not what any
count means.

**C109-4 — Context channel (Part 3).** Under the
registration filed at
`records/registration-2026-11-27.md` §4, the context
channel joins exactly the registered inputs — the
Spec 093 joined census (179 pairs, its single-site
limit carried on the channel's face), the tracked
population files' per-seal site field over the
1,670-seal population, and no iconography input while
the iconography sub-channel stands gated — and context
enters candidate scoring only as the registered
additive evidence term, capped by its own
context-label permutation null, with every context
score this study files labelled as carrying no
iconography component for as long as the sub-channel
is gated.

*Falsifier:* a context score computed from any input
outside the registered joins; an iconography input
used while Spec 100's gate stands closed, or used
under any terms other than the Holdat terms of
spec.md §6; a context term lifting a candidate beyond
its null's cap; a filed context score missing its
no-iconography label while the sub-channel is gated;
the Spec 093 census's single-site limit dropped from
any record that uses the census.

*Does not establish:* that context carries decipherment
evidence at these sizes — the channel makes context
scoreable under a null, and its own registered outcome
may be that the context term discriminates nothing,
which is filed as such.

**C109-5 — Meroitic gloss induction (Part 4).** Under
the registration filed at
`records/registration-2026-11-27.md` §7, gloss
induction executes only after Spec 102's digitisation
has landed and its freeze exists; it consumes
Spec 102's frozen filtered vocabulary and family sets
as filed, changes neither gate G-1 nor gate G-2, and
returns ranked gloss candidates per form scored by
prediction on the frozen partition's 33 held-out texts
under permutation nulls and the wrong-family control;
if Spec 102 closes NOT ADMINISTERED, this part files
NOT ADMINISTERED — INPUT GATE UNMET as its outcome and
does not execute.

*Falsifier:* gloss induction executed before
Spec 102's freeze exists, or on any vocabulary other
than the frozen one; a gate of Spec 102 amended,
waived, or routed around by this study; vocabulary
sourced elsewhere after a NOT ADMINISTERED close; a
candidate scored on development material under a
held-out label; the wrong-family control discriminating
as strongly as the target family without the part
closing under kill criterion 3.

*Does not establish:* any affiliation verdict — that
remains Spec 102's alone under its gates — and no
gloss: a ranked gloss candidate is a candidate reading
entering the program's candidate adjudication
machinery, not a meaning.

**C109-6 — Eteocypriot reading candidates (Part 5).**
Under the registration filed at
`records/registration-2026-11-27.md` §6, the
Eteocypriot generator composes candidate readings for
the ICS texts from the transliteration and the
registered comparanda only, and ranks them by the
registered rule (comparanda coverage; distributional
fit; prediction of the held-out forms at form level),
filing a ranked candidate set per text with margins —
and no record of this part carries licensing language,
resolves or presumes OD-O-2, or states or implies that
any check has been passed for the Eteocypriot program
as a whole.

*Falsifier:* a candidate composed from comparanda
outside the registered set; a ranking not equal to the
registered rule's mechanical consequence over the filed
candidate records; a licensing statement, an "all
checks passed" statement, or any resolution or
presumption of OD-O-2 anywhere in this part's records;
a candidate presented as a reading of a text rather
than as a ranked candidate.

*Does not establish:* anything about the Eteocypriot
language, its affiliation, or the correctness of any
candidate — the pilot's prediction floor failed on this
corpus for reasons no ranking removes, and this part's
output is a ranked set whose members stand or fall
under the program's existing adjudication, if they are
ever filed to it.

**C109-7 — Preflight discipline.** No generator's
output on an undeciphered system is scored under this
study before that generator has passed the conformance
preflight of spec.md §2 as registered at
`records/registration-2026-11-27.md` §2: synthetic
ground-truth recovery at the registered floors;
the deciphered-control clause in its R1 form, with the
Latin/Meroitic positional limitation carried on the
record's face rather than passed silently; and exact
two-implementation agreement on every ground-truth set
and recovery figure. A preflight failure or a
NOT MEASURABLE clause is filed as the leg's outcome,
and the generator produces no scoreable output until a
revised generator passes under a new registration.

*Falsifier:* any score computed on a generator's
undeciphered-system output before its preflight record
is filed as passed; a preflight record claiming a
positional recovery figure for the Latin or Meroitic
controls from the re-specified ground-truth sets
cycle 2 filed (1 mark and 0 marks against the floor
of 5); a recovery figure on which the two
implementations disagree, filed as a pass.

*Does not establish:* that a generator passing its
preflight will recover anything on an undeciphered
system — the preflight establishes that the generator
can recover truth where truth is known and measurable,
which is a necessary condition for trusting its
output, not a sufficient one.

**C109-8 — Provenance and replay.** Every candidate
this study files carries, in its candidate record, its
full generation provenance — generator identity and
version pin, search state or state hash, seed, the
data pins of every input, and the constraint-set
version — and every filed result is reproducible by
replay from its candidate records exactly (the same
candidate set, the same margins), as exercised by
TC-109-03 at each stage that files candidates.

*Falsifier:* a filed candidate with any provenance
field missing; a replay producing a different candidate
set or different margins from the filed ones; a result
filed from candidate records held anywhere other than
the custody home of D-109-4 with their pins in the
outcome record.

*Does not establish:* that a replayable candidate is a
correct one — provenance makes generation auditable
and repeatable; it is not evidence for any candidate's
content.

**C109-9 — Trace and standing discipline.** This
study's landings move no standing, verdict, or tally in
any program; every standings effect this study ever
records is licensed by a filed outcome record under an
existing gate's own registration (Spec 098's gate for
Part 1's candidates; the anchor and candidate
adjudication machinery for Parts 2, 4, and 5's outputs;
no gate at all for Part 3's scores, which are inputs to
other parts' scoring and nothing more), and the study's
stage-close records state, per program, what moved and
under which licensing record, or that nothing moved.

*Falsifier:* a standing, verdict, tally, anchor, or
class altered in content by any landing of this study
outside a licensing outcome record of an existing gate;
a stage-close record claiming an effect no licensing
record supports; a candidate of this study acquiring a
standing other than through the program's standing
machinery acting on a filed gate outcome.

*Does not establish:* that the standings after this
study's stages are correct — only that they are exactly
the standings the licensed outcomes produce, no more
and no less.
